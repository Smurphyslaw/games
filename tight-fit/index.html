Packing slip

  1. Every day there's one box with a country's outline cut into the floor. Same box for everyone.
  2. Drag countries off the cutting mat into the outline. Tap a piece to turn it. On a computer, scroll over it or press Q and E.
  3. Every country is cut at its true size, so no map tricks. Sri Lanka really is that big.
  4. Pieces can overlap and hang over the edge, but only what's inside the cut line counts.
  5. Happy with it? Press Pack it. One box a day, then watch how Pat packed it, play past boxes from the archive, or try practice boxes.
